Our Story

We all looked up

StarGazing was born from a single moment — a kid pointing at Saturn through a telescope for the first time, eyes wide with wonder. We make clothes for that feeling. Whether you're five years old or fifty, whether you trace constellations or just wish on the brightest light in the sky, there's something here for you.

From our Astronomy Edition's vintage badge aesthetic to the bold, dreamful art of the Wish Edition — every piece is made for people who still look up.
