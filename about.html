The Full Story

Born under a South African sky

StarGazing started with a single moment that probably feels familiar: a kid outside at night, neck craned upward, asking a parent what that bright one is. The answer was Saturn. The kid didn't stop asking questions for weeks. That kid grew up, kept looking up, and eventually wanted to make clothes that captured that feeling.

We're a South African clothing brand, and the sky looks extraordinary from down here. On a clear night in the Karoo, the Milky Way hangs overhead like someone spilled light across the dark. That image — void navy-black, scattered with gold — became the brand. It's the colour of our packaging, our website, and the lining of every hoodie we make.

There are two kinds of people who look up. The first kind makes a wish. They don't necessarily know what the bright object is — they just know it's beautiful and that pointing at it and hoping feels important. We made the Wish Edition for them. Bold statement art. Designs that say: I believe in something bigger than this moment.

The second kind brings a red-light torch and a star chart. They know exactly what they're looking at. They've memorised Messier objects and can name the moons of Jupiter from memory. The Astronomy Edition is for them — vintage badge-style graphics, muted palette, the kind of design that earns a compliment from someone who actually knows what a transit of Mercury is.

And then there's the Little Explorers collection — because the most important people looking up are the ones who haven't learned to stop yet. Kids deserve clothes that match their sense of wonder: cartoonish, bold, a little bit rocket-fuelled.

Founder's Note

Why we exist

I started StarGazing because I couldn't find a clothing brand that took astronomy seriously as an aesthetic — one that wasn't either too kitschy or too scientific. I wanted something that felt like standing outside at 2am watching a meteor shower: a little wonder, a little cold, completely worth it.

"The universe doesn't care whether you've memorised the HR diagram or whether you just think stars are pretty. Both are completely valid reasons to look up. StarGazing is for both."

— Founder, StarGazing

We're a small operation. Every design goes through more rounds of feedback than feels strictly necessary. The badge graphic on our logo took three weeks and forty-something iterations. We're not trying to be the biggest cosmic brand — we're trying to be the most considered one.

All products are printed on quality blanks, ethically sourced. We ship across South Africa and believe the best way to build a brand is one good product at a time.

Our Mission

  • Make clothes that feel like looking up — for every age and every kind of stargazer.
  • Bridge the gap between wonder and knowledge, between dreamers and scientists.
  • Design with intention: every graphic, every colourway, every thread count considered.
  • Keep it South African — proudly made here, for a sky we know well.
  • Remind people that curiosity about the universe is always worth celebrating.

What we stand for

Three things we believe

✦

Wonder is for everyone

You don't need a telescope or a PhD to feel the scale of the universe. You just need to look up on a clear night and let yourself feel small for a moment.

🪐

Design should earn its place

Every graphic we make has a reason for existing. We don't put a planet on a shirt because planets are trending. We do it because that particular design says something worth saying.

🌙

Kids are our best customers

Not because they spend the most money — they don't — but because they haven't learned to be embarrassed about loving space yet. We want to keep that going as long as possible.
